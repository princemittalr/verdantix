Judging criteria, and how we score in each

30%Technical implementation

A real pipeline, not a mockup. YOLO runs on live video and images, anomaly models run on streaming sensor data, the twin updates over WebSocket, and the LLM uses tool calls on real twin state. Show model metrics (mAP, precision on leak events) on one slide.

25%Innovation & creativity

A what-if simulator ("shut AC in B-Block at 5 PM: −312 kWh/week, −₹2.5k, −0.22 t CO₂"), a copilot that writes facility work orders, sensor and camera fusion that cuts false alarms, and a block-vs-block green score leaderboard.

20%Practicality & impact

What we build

One web app with four screens, and every screen feeds the twin.

must

1 · Campus twin map

A 3D campus with extruded buildings (DSU footprints traced from OpenStreetMap or satellite imagery). Each block is colored by live status. Click a block to drill into its floors and rooms, with live energy, water, occupancy and waste readings.

must

2 · Vision feed

YOLO on CCTV-style clips and a live webcam. It detects overflowing bins and litter, and counts people. Fused with power data, it flags lights or AC running in an empty room. Every detection pins an alert to its location on the map.

must

3 · Sensor anomalies

Energy, water and occupancy streams are replayed in real time. Isolation Forest plus a forecast-residual model flag overnight base load, leaks (night flow and pressure drop) and HVAC drift, each with a severity and a cost per hour.

must

4 · GenAI copilot & reports

A chat agent with tools: get_block_status, list_alerts, simulate, create_work_order. One-click weekly sustainability report as a PDF with charts, ₹ and CO₂ figures, and actions ranked by payback.

wow

What-if simulator

Sliders for AC setpoint, a lights-off schedule, fixing a leak, and bin pickup frequency. The twin recomputes kWh, litres, ₹ and CO₂ instantly. This is the main Best Innovation feature.

wow

Green score leaderboard

Each block gets a 0–100 score per day, and blocks are ranked against each other to get students involved. Easy to build and judges remember it.

Cut list if you fall behind, in this order: leaderboard, 3D (fall back to a 2D SVG campus map), the PDF (fall back to a markdown report on screen). Never cut the live CV moment or the copilot.

What we carry over from Clean City

The team's earlier Clean City app already proved a full report-to-fix loop: Snap → Verify → Route → Prove. Verdantix brings that loop onto campus and closes it around the digital twin: Detect → Verify → Assign → Prove. We reuse ideas and lessons only. The 3-minute demo script

  1. 0:00 The problem. One slide: what a campus like DSU wastes in energy, water and waste each year, in ₹.
  2. 0:20 The twin. Open the 3D DSU map. "Every block, live." Click into a block and then a room.
  3. 0:45 Vision, live. Point the webcam at a bin with paper overflowing, or at an empty seat with the lights on. A pin appears on the twin.
  4. 1:15 Sensors. Fast-forward the clock to 2 AM. A water leak alert fires: "Night flow 340 L/h above baseline, ₹ per day, likely pipe segment".
  5. 1:45 Copilot. Ask: "What should facilities fix first this week?" It answers with a ranked list from real twin data, then creates a work order.
  6. 2:15 What-if. Drag the AC schedule slider. Savings in ₹ and CO₂ update live.
  7. 2:35 Report and close. Download the PDF. Show the ESP32 → MQTT path for a real rollout at DSU. Each member names their part.

Questions judges will probably ask

  • "Where's the DSU data?" Public datasets mapped onto DSU's layout; the ingest API takes real meters as-is. Show the MQTT endpoint.
  • "How accurate is it?" Leak precision and recall on BattLeDIM labels; YOLO mAP@50 on the held-out split.
  • "Does the LLM hallucinate?" It only answers from tool results and cites the alert IDs. Numbers come from code, not from the model.
  • "What does a real rollout cost?" Per-block sensor bill of materials, existing CCTV reused, payback in months.
